01 · Company Description

A warehouse, a team, and the systems behind every parcel.

Andes Fulfillment Solutions is a third-party logistics (3PL) company that handles e-commerce order fulfillment for online retailers across Peru.

Founded in Lima in 2015 by a group of former retail operations managers, the company grew by offering online brands what they could not easily build on their own: a reliable warehouse, trained staff, and systems that turn an online order into a packed, labeled parcel on its way to the customer.

Today Andes operates an 18,000 square-meter distribution center in the Lurín logistics corridor, south of Lima, serving 42 retail clients, from independent online boutiques to regional apparel, beauty, and electronics brands.
