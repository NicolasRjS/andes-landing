01 · Company Description

A warehouse, a team, and the systems behind every parcel.

Andes Fulfillment Solutions is a third-party logistics (3PL) company that handles e-commerce order fulfillment for online retailers across Peru.

Founded in Lima in 2015 by a group of former retail operations managers, the company grew by offering growing online brands something they could not easily build on their own: a reliable warehouse, trained staff, and systems that turn an online order into a packed, labeled parcel on its way to the customer.

Today Andes operates a single 18,000 m² distribution center in the Lurín logistics corridor, south of Lima, where it receives, stores, picks, packs, and dispatches orders for 42 retail clients, from independent online boutiques to regional apparel, beauty, and consumer electronics brands.

03 · Products and Services

Everything between the online order and the customer’s door.

Andes sells fulfillment as a service. Clients connect their online stores and marketplace accounts to Andes’ warehouse management system (WMS), so orders flow automatically into the warehouse without manual data entry.

  1. 01

    Receiving and storage

    Inbound receiving, quality inspection, and put-away of client inventory, with real-time stock visibility for the client.

  2. 02

    Pick-and-pack fulfillment Core service

    Picking each online order, packing it with client-branded or standard materials, and labeling it for shipment. The company’s core service and largest revenue source.

  3. 03

    Value-added services

    Kitting and bundling, gift wrapping, promotional inserts, and custom packaging for campaigns.

  4. 04

    Returns management

    Receiving, inspecting, and restocking or disposing of returned items according to each client’s policy.

  5. 05

    Carrier coordination

    Hand-off to partner last-mile carriers, with coverage to all 25 regions of Peru and same-day dispatch for orders in Metropolitan Lima received before 3:00 p.m.

  6. Orders flow from the client’s store into Andes’ WMS automatically, with no manual data entry.

According to the Peruvian Chamber of Electronic Commerce (CAPECE), e-commerce sales in Peru reached about US$8.7 billion in the first half of 2025, with volume growing 18% compared with the same period of 2024 (El Comercio, 2025). More than 330,000 Peruvian businesses now sell online (Mobile Time Latinoamérica, 2025), and payments research firm PCMI describes Peru as one of the fastest-growing e-commerce markets in Latin America, projecting growth of about 17% per year through 2027 (Payments and Commerce Market Intelligence [PCMI], n.d.).

Demand is also highly seasonal: during the November 2025 CyberWow campaign organized by IAB Perú, e-commerce sales volume rose more than 161% compared with the four preceding weeks (Revista Economía, 2025). For fulfillment providers, this means volumes that grow every year and spike sharply during a few campaign weeks, which puts constant pressure on warehouse capacity, labor, and accuracy.

06 · Financials

Growing revenue, narrowing margins.

Andes is privately held. Its revenue has grown about 35% per year for three consecutive years, driven by new clients and higher order volume from existing ones. Profit margins, however, have narrowed as the company has relied on overtime and temporary staff to keep up with demand.
